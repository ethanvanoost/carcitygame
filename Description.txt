                            🚗🏙  CAR CITY GAME  🏙🚗
              The endless multiplayer driving city that never stops growing
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Car City Game is a massive open-world multiplayer driving game that runs right
in your browser — no downloads, no installs, no waiting. One click and you are
standing in an endless procedurally generated city with roads, highways,
railways, canals, tram streets, an elevated metro, harbors on the coast,
islands out at sea, and thousands of other things to discover. Every player on
the server shares the same living world: you see them drive past, you chat with
them, you trade with them, you race them, and you can even sleep in the rooms
they own.

This is not a small game. Car City Game has been updated over 120 times, and
every update made the world bigger, richer and weirder. Here is everything
waiting for you inside.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 🚗  DRIVE ANYTHING, ANYWHERE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• An ENDLESS city — roads and highways stretch forever in every direction,
  with real intersections, traffic that honks at you, and random events.
• A whole garage of buyable vehicles, from a humble $20,000 starter car all
  the way up to a $300,000 speed machine — every car with its own real
  silhouette, lights, grilles, badges and exhausts.
• Paint your car any color, refuel at gas stations, repair crash damage, and
  even resell cars you're done with.
• Auto-drive with voice guidance, photo mode for the perfect shot, and a
  full-featured live map with search, route planning, teleports and
  "Nearest…" finders for everything in the world.
• Fires break out in houses — and real fire trucks come spray them down.
  Accidents summon ambulances. The city lives around you.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 ⚓🛶🚋🚇  A COMPLETE TRANSPORT NETWORK
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• HARBORS on the coast with gantry cranes, stacked containers and warehouses.
  Load 24 cargo crates onto your own sailable CARGO SHIP (they stack up
  visibly on deck!), sail to another harbor and unload — the farther you
  ship, the more you earn.
• CANALS dug below the city with small boats to float around in, and little
  arched stone bridges wherever roads, highways and railways cross the water.
• TRAM STREETS with real rails, wire poles and stops — hop on when a tram
  waits and ride it through the city.
• An elevated METRO on a pillared viaduct with real stations and platforms.
• A CAR FERRY to the deep-sea FERRY ISLANDS — lighthouses, palm trees, crabs,
  beach shops with exclusive Pearl dumplings, and buried treasure marked
  with an X.
• Everything runs on a shared clock, so every player sees the same tram, the
  same metro and the same ferry at the same moment.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 💰  A REAL PLAYER-DRIVEN ECONOMY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Claim your own MARKET PLOT and build a real shop: tables, store shelves,
  display cases, custom building colors, even a purple spired market hall.
• Sell literally anything you own — dumplings, butter, phones, consoles,
  tablets, computers, Minecraft blocks, even items you invented yourself.
• Set your own prices, run TIMED BONUS SALES (10–75% off with a live
  countdown every buyer can see), and edit prices whenever you want.
• Your shop stays open even while you are OFFLINE — other players buy from
  it and the shared SOLD ledger makes sure stock really disappears for
  everyone the instant it sells.
• "Generate Shop" auto-builds a whole store from your collection, laying
  out shelves and display cases by value, with category tickboxes and fair
  round-robin mixing.
• Roadside BUYERS every ~500 m purchase your phones, consoles, tablets,
  computers, dumplings and butter at their real worth — with brand, model
  and color filters.
• Pay other players directly through the online inbox, send gifts to
  friends, and win money pots in multiplayer races.

 🎁  UNBOXING MADNESS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 ⚡  CREATE YOUR OWN ITEMS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• INVENT anything: name your item, describe what it DOES and how it LOOKS,
  and the game reads your words and gives it real powers.
• "Sushi that gives +80 food" becomes actually edible. Fuel words refill
  your tank. Repair words fix your car. Teleport words warp you across the
  map.
• Manufacture copies for $1,000 each and sell them at your market — the
  design is saved forever, so sold-out stock is just one click from
  restocking. Buyers receive the item AND its powers.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 🏠  OWN THE CITY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  MANSIONS with full furniture and garden editors.
• One owner per property on the whole server — when you own it, everyone
  sees a golden "Your Name's Room" plaque on the sign, in the walk-up text

• The 🛏 ROOMS map lists every player's properties on the server, grouped

• Open a dumpling shop inside your mansion, keep a guest book, and let

 ⛏🎮🎹  AND SO MUCH MORE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• MINECRAFT-style caves: mine wood, stone, coal, iron, gold and glowing

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 🌐  TRUE SHARED-WORLD MULTIPLAYER
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Every server is one persistent world. Players see each other live on the road
