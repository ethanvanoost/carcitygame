                            🚗🏙  CAR CITY GAME  🏙🚗
              The endless multiplayer driving city that never stops growing
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Car City Game is a massive open-world multiplayer driving game that runs right
in your browser — no downloads, no installs, no waiting. Pick a username and
you are standing in an endless procedurally generated city with roads,
highways, railways, canals, tram streets, an elevated metro, harbors on the
coast, islands out at sea, and thousands of other things to discover. Every
player on the server shares the same living world: you see them drive past,
you chat with them, you trade with them, you race them, and you can even ride
along in the passenger seat of their car.

This is not a small game. Car City Game has been updated over 120 times, and
every update made the world bigger, richer and weirder. Here is everything
waiting for you inside.
